# Joseph Aarón Atencio Arias

Economics student at the University of Costa Rica, focused on econometrics and data analysis. I work with Python, R and Stata on household surveys, macroeconomic series and financial data, and in economic consulting I prepared economic outlook reports and market analyses for clients across Central America. I am interested in data analysis, banking, finance and economic research.

Each project below includes its code, its data and a reproducible report. The reports are written in Spanish.

## Projects

### [The macroeconomic trilemma in Costa Rica and the role of international reserves, 1999-2025](https://github.com/josephatencio/trilema-reservas-costa-rica)

With Marco Oconitrillo González. Quarterly indices of monetary independence, exchange rate stability and financial openness built from the raw data, with the trilemma constraint estimated for each exchange rate regime and automated tests that reproduce every figure in the paper. [Paper in PDF](https://github.com/josephatencio/trilema-reservas-costa-rica/blob/main/report/trilema_reservas_costa_rica.pdf), in Spanish.

### [U.S. equity investment portfolios, 2003-2026](https://github.com/josephatencio/carteras-inversion-acciones-eeuu)

A cap-weighted index of 44 stocks and 11 sector portfolios used to study diversification, the efficient frontier, the Sharpe and Black versions of the CAPM and the cost of capital with the Fama, French and Carhart model. [Report in PDF](https://github.com/josephatencio/carteras-inversion-acciones-eeuu/blob/main/report/carteras_inversion_acciones_eeuu.pdf), in Spanish.

### [Motherhood and women's employment in Costa Rica, 2010-2025](https://github.com/josephatencio/maternidad-empleo-costa-rica)

A pseudo-event study with sixteen National Household Surveys that measures how much women's employment changes after their first child compared with men's, and whether the penalty is smaller for those who delay motherhood. [Report in PDF](https://github.com/josephatencio/maternidad-empleo-costa-rica/blob/main/report/maternidad_empleo_costa_rica.pdf), in Spanish.
